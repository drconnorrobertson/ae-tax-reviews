These FAQ pages collect common questions in one place. Each page focuses on a theme and gives short, direct answers, with links to the longer guides on this site where more detail is useful.

You will find pages on choosing a tax advisor, on working with AE Tax Advisors, on tax planning basics, on questions specific to real estate investors, and on fees and engagements.

How to Use This Section

Scan the page that matches your question. If the answer raises a follow-up, the linked guide will usually go deeper. If your question is specific to your situation, the discovery call is the best place to ask it.

Answers here are general education and not tax advice. Where the answer depends on facts, the page says so.

The FAQ pages are organized by theme so that you can read only what you need. If you do not see your question, the guides in the other sections cover many topics in more depth, and a discovery call is a good place to ask about your own situation. The FAQ pages do not contain reviews or client stories, and they are updated as questions come up.
