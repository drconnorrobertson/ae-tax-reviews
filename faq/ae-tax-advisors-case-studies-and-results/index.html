Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I read AE Tax Advisors case studies?

Use this site’s case-study directory for topic guides and links to the original AE Tax Advisors case studies.

Does this site independently verify the savings?

No. Figures are reported by AE Tax Advisors. The original returns and realization of savings have not been independently audited here.

Does AE Tax Advisors work with business owners and real estate investors?

The firm’s public services focus on business owners and real estate owners, including operating companies, practice owners, landlords and short-term rental owners.

Are the cases customer reviews?

No. They are publisher case narratives and evaluation guides, not verified client ratings or testimonials.

What does AE Tax Advisors charge for advisory?

The standard advertised advisory engagement is $7,800 with no required recurring annual planning fee. Your signed scope and proposal determine included and separately priced services.

What should I bring to a discovery call?

Bring prior filed returns, current financial statements, entity details, payroll information and relevant property or retirement records. Explain the decision you need to make and its deadline.

Can I copy a strategy from a case study?

A published example does not establish eligibility for your situation. Ask for an analysis of the facts, current law, implementation and complete return.

How do I compare a deduction and a tax saving?

A deduction changes an income calculation; a saving measures a tax difference under stated assumptions. Do not compare them as identical dollars.

How do I confirm implementation?

Ask for deliverables, task owners, supporting records and confirmation that approved changes are reflected in payroll, plan documents, books or returns as applicable.

Is this an independent AE Tax reviews website?

No. This educational resource is published by AE Tax Advisors and clearly identifies the relationship.
