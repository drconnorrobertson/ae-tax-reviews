Airline Captain Saves $78,000 with Per Diem and Real Estate Strategy

Scott
Dallas, TX • High-Income Professional
Tax Savings
$78,000
Strategy Used
Per Diem Optimization, Rental Real Estate, STR Offset Strategy
Rating
5 / 5 Stars

I have been flying for a major airline for twenty years and doing my own taxes the entire time. I figured a W-2 pilot with no business had limited tax planning options. AE Tax Advisors showed me how wrong I was.

They started by optimizing my per diem and travel expense strategy. As a pilot, I receive per diem payments from my airline, but the tax treatment of those payments and the interaction with my actual travel costs was something I had been handling incorrectly. AE Tax sorted it out and identified adjustments going back two years.

The bigger play was real estate. They helped me acquire two rental properties -- a short-term rental near a popular vacation spot and a long-term rental near the airport. My wife manages the STR and qualifies under the material participation rules, so the accelerated depreciation from a cost segregation study offsets my W-2 income. The LTR provides steady cash flow and additional depreciation benefits.

AE Tax also knew the specific IRS rules for airline crew -- domicile versus home base tax deductions, uniform and equipment costs, union dues, and the distinction between per diem and actual expenses. They even knew about the FAA medical exam deduction. That level of industry-specific knowledge gave me confidence.

Total first-year savings: $78,000. The combination of travel expense optimization and real estate tax strategy turned a six-figure tax bill into something much more manageable.

If you are a pilot or airline professional, AE Tax Advisors understands your specific situation. The savings are there -- you just need the right team to find them.
