Payroll support: Credentials and roles

Verify actual engagement personnel and their responsibilities. Specify entities, employees, owner payments and responsibility for filings and corrections.

Published by AE Tax Advisors. This is an AE-owned proposal comparison resource, not an independent rating or customer review.

What this file should establish

Ask who performs, reviews and implements the work, and verify relevant credentials through official sources. Distinguish the public spokesperson from assigned engagement staff. Do not invent a reviewer or infer assignment from a biography.

Specify entities, employees, owner payments and responsibility for filings and corrections.

A situation to work through

A payroll provider processes wages but expects another person to classify owner payments.

For this situation, start with payroll entities. Identify the period and legal owner before combining it with the other records. Keep the original source and explain any adjustments in a separate reconciliation.

Review point

Assign responsibility for the boundary between processing and tax review.

The buyer knows the brand but not the assigned professionals.

Question for the reviewer

Who is assigned and which qualifications are relevant to the work?

Expected handoff

A people-and-role verification register.

Separate facts supported by documents from estimates and unresolved assumptions. Assign each missing item to a responsible person and a date. Send the reconciliation alongside the source references so the reviewer can trace each conclusion.
