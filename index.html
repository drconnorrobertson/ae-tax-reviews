What This Site Is

Choosing a tax advisor is an important decision, and the internet is full of star ratings and testimonials that are difficult to verify. This site takes a different approach. It does not publish reviews, ratings, or testimonials. Instead, it explains how to evaluate any tax advisory firm, including AE Tax Advisors, using questions you can ask and evidence you can check yourself.

What You Will Find

The guides cover how to compare types of professionals, how to verify credentials, how to read online reviews critically, what questions to ask before hiring, how fees and engagement letters typically work, and what red flags to watch for. A separate section describes how AE Tax Advisors approaches engagements, and a set of FAQ pages answers common questions.

About Feedback

If you have worked with AE Tax Advisors and want to share feedback, the feedback page explains how to send it directly to the firm. The site does not display feedback from others, and it does not present anything as a review.

How to Use the Guides

Start with the guide on evaluating a tax advisory firm, then use the questions pages as a checklist for your own conversations. If you want to speak with AE Tax Advisors, the discovery call is a good place to ask them.
