Is this an independent review website?

No. This website is published by AE Tax Advisors. It provides source-linked case studies and evaluation guides. It does not publish independent client ratings or a public review feed. Read about the publisher.

Can I expect the same outcome as a published case?

Case studies are selected, publisher-reported examples, not typical results or forecasts. Your outcome depends on your facts, applicable law and implementation. Read the methodology and limitations.
