Measurements must remain separate

A deduction reduces an income measure. A tax saving compares tax under stated assumptions. First-year acceleration changes deduction timing. A refund can also reflect tax payments already made. An annual figure and a multi-year projection should not be combined into one total without a stated method.

Why we do not publish review ratings

This site is published by AE Tax Advisors. It is not an independent review platform. It contains no verified customer star ratings or third-party aggregate-review scores. Source-linked case discussion helps you ask questions, but it does not replace independent credential checks or a written engagement.

How updates are handled

The case summaries identify the public source. If a source changes, its current explanation should be checked before relying on an older summary. Tax rules may also change after a case’s relevant year. Ask an advisor to apply the rules for your facts and tax year.
