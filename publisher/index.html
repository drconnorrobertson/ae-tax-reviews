The publisher and the advisory firm

AE Tax Advisors publishes educational material for business owners and real estate investors. This website is part of that publishing work. The firm's firm and team information, pricing information and written engagement terms are the appropriate places to assess who will perform services and what the engagement includes.

How this website relates to AE Tax Advisors

This is an AE Tax Advisors publication website. It provides firm-published case studies and engagement information. It is not an independent review platform, and the case collection is selected by the publisher. Readers evaluating the firm should consider independent sources and verify the credentials and scope relevant to their engagement.

Use a case study as a question, not a prediction

A published outcome can help identify an issue worth discussing, but it does not establish a prospective client's savings. Compare the taxpayer's income, ownership, property use, year, implementation and measurement period. Read the methodology before comparing headline figures.

The site links to the original AE case-study sources and separates reported outcomes from explanatory examples. Underlying returns and realized savings have not been independently audited for this publication.
